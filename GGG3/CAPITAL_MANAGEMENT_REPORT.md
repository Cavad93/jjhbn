## Формулы управления капиталом

### Открытие LONG позиции:
```
exec_price = market_price × (1 + slippage)
cost = exec_price × amount
commission = cost × commission_rate
total_cost = cost + commission
balance_new = balance_old - total_cost
```

### Закрытие LONG позиции:
```
exec_price = market_price × (1 - slippage)
revenue = exec_price × amount
commission = revenue × commission_rate
received = revenue - commission
pnl = received - (entry_cost + entry_commission)
balance_new = balance_old + received
```
